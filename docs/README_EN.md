# SENTINELA-SOBERANO-2037

## Digital Prophecy

In a not-so-distant future, the sovereignty of nations will be tested not by conventional armies, but by digital threats that transcend physical borders. The SENTINELA-SOBERANO-2037 project emerges from the vision to anticipate and neutralize these futuristic crimes, protecting Brazil's natural resources and critical infrastructure.

### Predicted Crimes

*   **Neuro-jacking:** The manipulation of brain-machine interfaces for mind control or information extraction.
*   **Quantum Sabotage:** Attacks using quantum computing to break encryptions and destabilize critical systems.
*   **Resource Theft:** The exploitation and diversion of natural resources through autonomous networks and artificial intelligence.

## Sovereignty Manifesto

Brazil, through Impulso Digital Soluções em I.A., rises as the master creator of technology, forging an impenetrable digital shield. Our mission is to ensure that Brazilian technological sovereignty is unshakeable, immune to any external interference. We will not be mere consumers of technology, but the architects of our own digital destiny, protecting our nation and its resources for future generations.
